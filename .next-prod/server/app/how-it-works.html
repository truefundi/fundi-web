From problem to fixed in four steps

Fundi coordinates the whole job, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Tell us what is wrong

    Pick a service, describe the problem, add photos and your location, and choose how urgent it is.

  2. 2

    Fundi finds who can fix it

    We match you with a qualified technician nearby who is available now, and show you how long it will take them to arrive.

  3. 3

    Diagnose and approve

    The technician inspects the problem and sends a clear quote. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

  4. 4

    Repair, settle and rate

    The work gets done, you record how you paid, and you rate the service. Every job is saved.
